Context

During a period of rapid hiring at Tradexa, the HR team needed support managing recruitment pipelines, candidate coordination, and onboarding for new technical and non-technical hires.

My role

HR Intern supporting talent acquisition and people operations. I coordinated screening, maintained candidate records, and assisted with onboarding documentation.

Tools

WorkdayMonday.comGoogle WorkspaceSlack

Goals

  • Support the talent acquisition team in screening and shortlisting candidates for open roles.
  • Ensure all new hire onboarding materials were complete, accurate, and delivered on time.
  • Maintain organized candidate tracking and communication records throughout each hiring cycle.

Approach

I took ownership of the early-stage screening pipeline: reviewing resumes, conducting initial outreach, and scheduling interviews for hiring managers. I maintained recruitment data and a candidate tracker that gave the team real-time visibility into where each candidate stood.

For onboarding, I compiled and standardized the documentation package for new hires, so every new team member had what they needed before day one and the full-time HR team carried less admin.

Outcomes

  • Screened 15+ candidates across technical and non-technical roles, shortlisting for 3 successful hires.
  • 100% of onboarding documentation completed and delivered ahead of each start date.
  • Structured pipeline reports for leadership that supported staffing decisions.
  • A reusable candidate tracker adopted by the team for later hiring cycles.

Key takeaway

People operations runs on trust. Candidates, hiring managers, and new hires all depend on someone following through. The best processes are invisible: when onboarding is seamless, new hires feel set up for success from day one.
