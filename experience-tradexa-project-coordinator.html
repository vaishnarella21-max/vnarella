Context

Tradexa is a B2B technology platform for global trade. As Project Coordinator, I was responsible for the timely delivery of technical projects and for bridging the gap between client requirements and engineering execution.

My role

Project Coordinator and primary client liaison. I led a team of 27 and owned stakeholder communication, requirements intake, delivery tracking, and reporting across assigned accounts.

Tools

JiraSlackMeta Business SuiteGoogle Workspace

Goals

  • Deliver every client-facing technical project on time.
  • Reduce ambiguity in requirements before development began, minimizing rework and scope creep.
  • Streamline communication between external stakeholders and internal engineering teams.

Approach

I implemented a structured intake process that required all critical details to be captured and signed off before a ticket moved to development. This reduced late-stage clarification requests by removing ambiguity at the source.

I established daily stand-ups, weekly progress reports, and a blockers log visible to both the client and the engineering lead, creating a shared source of truth that removed guesswork from status updates. By the end of my tenure, the process had become the team's standard operating model.

Outcomes

  • 100% on-time delivery across 10+ concurrent projects and 32 client websites for brands including Saucony, Hyperice, Garmin, 2XU, Nikon, and Godox.
  • Turnaround time cut by 30% through structured scheduling and delivery tracking.
  • $50K client contract renewed following consistently high delivery standards and relationship management.
  • Performance dashboards and reports for leadership from engagement data across Meta Business Suite.

Key takeaway

Successful project management is mostly communication. Clear expectations and proactive updates prevent most issues before they become critical. A good coordinator doesn't just track tasks. They remove the conditions that create problems in the first place.
