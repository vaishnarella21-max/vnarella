Context

MAG is the regional planning agency for the Phoenix metro area. I work in its communications team and also support QA and testing for IT, which is not my main focus.

My role

Digital Media Intern. I build monthly content calendars, track social performance daily, build reporting dashboards, and wrote a QA plan for an internal program management system.

Tools

Sprout SocialGovDeliveryClarityCritical MentionExcelAI-assisted build

Goals

  • Replace a slow manual monthly engagement report with something faster and more trustworthy.
  • Understand which outreach efforts actually drive engagement, and why.
  • Plan content around what the data shows.
  • Help IT catch usability and accessibility issues before a system launches.

Approach

The monthly report pulled from separate GovDelivery, Clarity, and all-staff survey exports and took a full day. I built an AI-assisted dashboard that reads the raw exports in one place, and ran validation passes against the source numbers. That is how I found and diagnosed a GovDelivery open-rate tracking anomaly.

For social, I analyzed 97 posts across 7 outreach efforts to compare engagement rates, and built a Critical Mention media-monitoring dashboard that flags one-time coverage spikes so they are not mistaken for trends. For QA, I wrote the plan for a program management system's landing page and header search.

Outcomes

  • A full-day manual monthly report replaced by an AI-assisted dashboard serving 120 employees.
  • A 3.4x engagement-rate gap isolated (6.69% vs. 1.99%) and presented with recommendations to the Communications team.
  • Data quality protected. A GovDelivery data anomaly diagnosed and validated, and one-time media spikes separated from real trends.
  • Content planning on data. Monthly calendars built from prior-month performance, with results tracked daily in Sprout Social.
  • A QA plan delivered: test plan, test cases, UAT script, accessibility checklist, and a living bug log.

Key takeaway

Trusting a number comes before using it. Validating the data first, then recommending, is what makes a dashboard useful instead of just fast.
